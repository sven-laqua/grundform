Grundform

Grundform

A visual identity for mobile apps built from three shapes, three hues, one geometric typeface and a black rule.

Drawn from the 1923 Weimar exhibition poster tradition, Kandinsky's form-and-colour correspondence (circle–blue, square–red, triangle–yellow), and the black-grid structure of De Stijl. Everything below is a token you can lift into a design system; the phone mock-ups at the end show the tokens in use in both themes.

Principles

  • Shape carries meaning. Circle, square and triangle are the icon and status vocabulary. A circle is a state or a person, a square is a thing or a place, a triangle is a signal or a warning. Colour reinforces shape; it never substitutes for it.
  • Black rules, not shadows. Structure is drawn with 2px black (or paper-coloured in dark mode) lines. Elevation is expressed by surface tone, never by drop shadow or blur.
  • Rectangles stay rectangular. Only elements derived from the circle — avatars, FABs, toggles, pills — are rounded, and then fully. There is no in-between radius on containers.
  • Paper, not white. The light canvas is warm paper; pure white is reserved for raised surfaces so cards read as sheets laid on the page.
  • One loud thing per screen. Primaries are saturated and rare. A screen carries one primary-coloured block or one accent action, not both.
  • Blue acts, red alerts. Blue is the action colour; red is reserved for emphasis, destructive actions and errors; yellow warns and highlights. Green sits outside the Bauhaus trio and is admitted only for success states.

Typography

One family, Jost — a geometric sans in the Futura lineage that the Bauhaus workshops used. Fallback stack: Futura, Century Gothic, Avenir, then system sans. Weight and size do the work; there are no italics in the system and no all-caps labels. Display sizes use tight tracking; body text is set loose enough to read on a small screen at arm's length.

Shape and spacing

Radius 0

Cards, sheets, buttons, list containers, images. The default.

Radius 4

Text inputs and code chips only — enough to read as fillable, not enough to read as soft.

Radius full

Avatars, FABs, toggles, segmented controls, tag pills. Anything born from the circle.

Rules: 2px in ink colour for structural borders (cards, tabs, nav). 1px hairline for row dividers inside a container. No shadows.

Spacing: a 4pt base scale — 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64. Screen gutters are 16; card padding is 16; section gaps are 24. Touch targets are 48pt minimum, buttons 48 tall.

Iconography: build icons from the three primitives on a 24pt grid with 2pt strokes and flat ends. Filled shapes for selected state, outlined for unselected. Status glyphs follow the shape vocabulary: circle for success or presence, square for a stopped or neutral item, triangle for a warning.

Components in both themes

The same screen rendered against each token set. The circle-and-square FAB, black-rule tabs and paper-on-black inversion are the recognisable moves; everything else stays quiet.

Component notes

  • Buttons. Square-cornered, 48pt tall, 2px border in ink. Primary fills with blue; destructive fills with red; secondary is outline only. The pill variant is for filter and chip-like actions, never for the main call to action.
  • Tabs. Text on a 2px black rule; the active tab carries a 4px blue underline inside the rule. No pill or segmented tabs at the top of a screen.
  • Cards. White (or #1B1B1B) sheets with a 2px rule. One card per screen may be a colour block; that card is the screen's single loud element.
  • Lists. Rows inside a ruled container, divided by hairlines. Leading glyph is a filled primitive coloured by category.
  • Alerts. Full-width ruled bar; a glyph in the shape vocabulary leads the message. Warning inverts to yellow with black text; errors and successes keep the surface colour and colour only the glyph.
  • FAB. Red circle, 56pt, bottom right. It is the only red fill on a default screen.
  • Focus. 3px yellow outline with 2px offset, in both themes.
